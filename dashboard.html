Half-up rounding vs. Python's banker's rounding

Python's built-in round() uses round-half-to-even, so round(0.5) = 0. Indian financial accounting requires round-half-up instead. The anchor case below is drawn directly from batch STL_2026_08 — it's the reason the three E04 exceptions in that batch carry a 1-paise drift.

Gross amount133,749 paise (₹1,337.49)
Platform fee @ 200 bps133,749 × 200 ÷ 10,000 = 2,674.98 → 2,675 paise
18% GST on fee2,675 × 1,800 ÷ 10,000 = 481.50 paise
Banker's rounding: 481.50 → 481 paise
Ledgerscope half-up: 481.50 → 482 paise — matches the batch exactly
